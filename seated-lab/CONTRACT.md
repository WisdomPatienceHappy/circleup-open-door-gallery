# From your seat · demo interactions

This is a local-state photo simulation. No live call, room, account or direction-sharing service is connected.

The default Panoramic seat view keeps a fixed camera seat and fixed world circle. Drag to look; pinch or +/− changes view width. Release every finger to spring toward the current speaker. Keyboard arrows look, +/− zoom and0faces speaker. Choose3–18people including yourself.

The video centers and bottom guide follow the same staged curve, moving together through pan. Seating curve adjusts its strength. This curve is a presentation choice, not a literal equal-height-head projection.

Prevent circle overlap defaults On: equal apparent circles shrink to fit projected center gaps, without moving seats. Circle clearance controls the gap and the drawer shows actual fitted pixels. With fitting Off, Distance compensation compares natural size and equal apparent size.

Camera-facing keeps round circles. Shared turns rotates the whole image surface with simulated participant direction; it does not alter eyes or source pixels. The angular plane effect is a local affine approximation. Share my direction is Off by default and only changes the local observer preview.

The older Perspective seat and Stable circle overview are comparison options. Literal pinhole perspective can distort spacing; its projective turned surfaces may overlap. The steady angular default is the intended no-overlap comparison.

Play/Next and rapid conversation are simulations. Mic/Camera/Request/Leave are local demonstrations. Reset all restores defaults and stops sharing/playback.
